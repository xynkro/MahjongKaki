# YouTube drafts: unit compilations (not approved, not published)

Videos: media-src/compilations/<n>-<unit>.mp4, built by scripts/compile-unit.sh.
First draft from Codex, checked against the engine rules and edited by Claude, 12 Nov 2026.

1. **Singapore Mahjong: Meet the tiles** (1:26)
   Get to know the three suits, the winds and the dragons, and the flowers and animals that score bonus tai.
   Part 1 of 7 in the Mahjong Kaki beginner series.

2. **Singapore Mahjong: Pung, chow and pairs** (1:37)
   Learn to recognise a pung (three of a kind), a chow (three in a row) and a pair. See how four sets and one pair form a winning hand of 14 tiles.
   Part 2 of 7 in the Mahjong Kaki beginner series.

3. **Singapore Mahjong: Setting up the table** (3:32)
   Learn how to choose East and when the dealer stays or passes to the right. See how to build the wall and use the dice to find the split. Then follow the deal and learn where to draw your tiles.
   Part 3 of 7 in the Mahjong Kaki beginner series.

4. **Singapore Mahjong: Taking a turn and making a kong** (0:53)
   Learn the rhythm of a turn: draw one tile, throw one away. See how a kong (four of a kind) works and where its replacement tile comes from.
   Part 4 of 7 in the Mahjong Kaki beginner series.

5. **Singapore Mahjong: Claiming a discard** (1:17)
   Learn how to call pung and why you can only call chow from the player on your left. See how to claim a discarded tile to complete a winning hand.
   Part 5 of 7 in the Mahjong Kaki beginner series.

6. **Singapore Mahjong: Seat winds and round winds** (0:57)
   Learn what your seat wind means, how the round wind changes as play goes round the table, and how to tell the two apart.
   Part 6 of 7 in the Mahjong Kaki beginner series.

7. **Singapore Mahjong: Tai, scoring and payouts** (1:45)
   Learn what tai means and some common ways to earn it. Find out how each tai doubles the payout and who pays, including the discarder paying double.
   Part 7 of 7 in the Mahjong Kaki beginner series.
